Try Tessel

A Root and Step, with seven songs on Step. Each song brings its own units onto the row. Change anything you like: it all works, as on the real thing. Nothing is kept when you leave the page.

This works best on a computer. On a phone the units are very small, but you can still try it.

How to play

Buttons and keys
Click them. Shift-click a key to hold it down; click it again to let go.
Encoders
Drag up or down to turn one, or use the scroll wheel over it. To push one, click or tap it without moving; to hold the push, press it and keep still. Right-click pushes it too.
Your computer's keyboard
Z to M and Q to O play the keys of the unit in front; [ and ] are OCT- and OCT+.
Another unit
Click it in the row along the top to bring it forward. Every unit keeps playing.
Another song
On Step, press SONG, turn encoder 1 to the song, then push encoder 1 to load it.
