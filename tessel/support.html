The test

Every unit can test itself: every button, light and colour, the encoders, the sound, and the joins between units. It ends with PASS, or a list of what failed and a code to send us.

  • A new unit: push encoder 3, TEST, on the choose screen.
  • Any time after: SETUP, then the RESET page, then TEST. Your sounds are kept, and the unit goes back to what it was afterwards.
A step-by-step guide to the test, and what each result means, will be here.

If something's not right

No soundCheck the volume wheel, and that the headphones are in PHONES. LINE OUT is always at full level.
A unit is darkThe power supply can't run that many units: use a 3 A USB-C supply. A row holds eight units; a ninth stays dark.
NEW FIRMWAREThe Root has a newer version. Hold encoder 4 to update, or press anything else for later.
Something oddRun the test.
Start afreshSETUP, the RESET page, then SETTINGS (keeps your sounds) or EVERYTHING; hold encoder 4.
