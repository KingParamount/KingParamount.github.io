Tessel Volt

Mono synth for bass and lead

As it comes: the standard panel, with the button names written on by hand. Click to see it larger. The names to write on, to print (PDF).

Volt is really two synths in one. In CLASSIC mode it's a three-oscillator monosynth with a warm ladder filter, for fat basses and singing leads; in ACID mode it's a single oscillator through a squelching filter, with the accents and slides that acid house is built on.

  • CLASSIC: three oscillators, noise, a four-pole ladder filter, glide
  • ACID: one oscillator, a resonant filter that screams, accent and slide from Step
  • Every control named and ranged as on a classic panel
  • 47 factory sounds across both modes; four banks of your own

Learn it from the first sound to your own: Making Sounds, chapter 4.
