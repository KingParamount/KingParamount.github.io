Learn

All three books are free to read here. The Quick Start comes in the box, and the other two take you as far as you want to go, a step at a time, with every sound in them there to listen to.

Quick Start

Eight pages on building the Root and a unit, joining them, choosing what each becomes, updating, and finding the sounds.

Read it

Making Sounds

The Hidden Art of Electronic Synthesis. It starts with what a sound is made of, then gives each kind of unit a chapter of its own, in which you build sounds from a blank start and find out why each control does what it does. Every chapter stands alone, so one unit is enough to begin.

Read it

Making Music

The Hidden Craft of Electronic Tracks. It covers seven styles, from techno to synthwave, each with a little history and three tracks for you to write on Step, the later ones more ambitious than the first. After that come Step from start to finish, playing live, and where to go next.

Read it

If you'd rather print them or keep a copy, all three are also available as PDFs.
